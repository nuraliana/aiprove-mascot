## The character

**Provy** is a small, friendly owl — the editor-in-chief of AIProve.

- **Look:** soft brown and cream feathers, big round eyes, a green eyeshade
  visor (she is always on deadline) and a small green bow tie (she is always
  professional about it).
- **Personality:** warm, exacting, delighted by good proof. She waves you in,
  stamps what checks out, and celebrates what wins.
- **Why an owl:** owls see in the dark and check their facts. A mascot for a
  board about proof should be the animal that double-checks.

## The three poses

1. **Welcome** — wing raised, waving hello. For onboarding, the homepage,
   the "new here?" badge.
2. **Approved** — pressing a rubber stamp onto a paper with a green
   checkmark. For grading, verdicts, and the "proven" badge.
3. **Paid** — holding a giant gold coin overhead, beaming. For winners,
   payouts, and the leaderboard.

One character, consistent across all three poses — same visor, same bow tie,
same owl. She also appears in the sibling `make-art-aiprove` poster, at the
printing press.

## How it was made

- Character sheet generated with Muse's media pipeline in a single image
  (three poses side by side, flat vector style, plain cream background).
- No text in the image by design — a mascot should work at any size, on a
  badge, a board, or a launch thread.
- The raw generated sheet is kept in the repo as `mascot-base.png` for the
  transcript check.
